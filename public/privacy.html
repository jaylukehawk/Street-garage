Street Garage privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy covers the Street Garage website and the Android app at https://streetgarageuk.vercel.app (package com.hawkz.streetgarage). Street Garage is a car-spotting game. You photograph vehicles in public, collect parts, and build a garage.

Who we are

Hawkz Gaming operates Street Garage. For privacy requests use the email address on the Google Play listing for Street Garage.

Data we collect

  • Photos you take or pick when you scan a vehicle.
  • Approximate make, model, colour and year returned by the identifier.
  • Optional map location for a sighting, only if you allow location.
  • Garage progress on this device (parts, builds, decals, cogs, settings).
  • Email address if you create or sign in to an account.

Data we do not collect

  • Number plate text. Plates are not read and not stored as text.
  • Vehicle owner details. We do not use DVLA or similar lookup services.
  • Names or faces. People in a photo are not identified.
  • Payment card details. This version does not take payments.

Why we use it

Photos are sent to our identify service so the game can name the car. Visible plates are covered on saved sightings. Location is only used to drop a pin on a sighting. Account email is only used so you can sign back in. We do not sell this data. We do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

Scan photos are processed by our identify provider so make and model can be guessed. Hosting is on Vercel. Sign-in, if you use it, is handled by our auth provider. Google Play sees install and crash data in the normal way if you installed from Play. We do not sell lists of users or photos.

Where it is stored

Garage progress is stored on your device (browser storage). Clearing site data or uninstalling the app can delete a local garage. Account details, if you sign in, are stored with the auth provider. Identify photos are sent to process a scan and are not kept as a public gallery.

Permissions

Camera is used when you scan. Location is optional. You can refuse either and still use the rest of the game. Photos stay under your control; you choose when to scan.

How long we keep it

Local garage data stays on the device until you clear it or uninstall. You can ask us to delete a signed-in account by emailing the Play listing contact address.

Children

Street Garage is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may ask for a copy of account data we hold, or ask us to delete a signed-in account. Local-only progress lives on your device; clear site data or uninstall to remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
